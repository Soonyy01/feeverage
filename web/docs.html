Feeverage docs

Feeverage is a token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Every token it launches is wired to one leveraged perpetual position on Hyperliquid. Trading the token pays fees; the fees become margin; the margin is levered into the market the creator picked.

In one line: your token's fees, leveraged. $1 of fees at 5× is $5 of BTC exposure.

Launching a token

  1. Connect. Sign in with a wallet, email or social login. Feeverage uses Privy, so a wallet is created for you if you don't have one.
  2. Describe the token. Name, ticker, logo link, description and socials. All of it is stored on-chain in the token contract.
  3. Pick the position. A Hyperliquid market (BTC, ETH, HYPE, SOL, XRP, DOGE), long or short, and a leverage from 1× to 20×. Leverage is capped at the market's own maximum on Hyperliquid.
  4. Make the first buy. Launching includes your first buy in the same transaction, so nobody can buy before you. The minimum is 0.0001 ETH.
  5. Confirm. One transaction creates the token, its bonding curve and your buy. You pay the launch fee, your first buy and gas.

The strategy line

Feeverage appends a line to your description that records the position, for example:

Fees → 5x LONG BTC on Hyperliquid · feeverage:BTC:L:5

Because it is part of the token's on-chain metadata, the strategy is public and can't be changed after launch. The keeper reads this line to know what to trade.

The position

Each token has exactly one position: the market, side and leverage chosen at launch. Every time new fees arrive, the keeper adds them as margin and opens more size in the same direction.

new size = deposit × 0.95 × leverage ÷ mark price

The 5% kept aside covers Hyperliquid trading fees and funding so a fresh deposit doesn't immediately sit at the liquidation edge. Positions use cross margin, so all the margin in the token's account backs the position.

  • Orders are immediate-or-cancel limit orders at 1% through the mark price.
  • Hyperliquid's minimum order is $10 notional, so very small deposits wait until they add up.
  • The position is never closed by Feeverage. It runs until it is liquidated.

Fees

Every buy and sell of a Feeverage token pays a trading fee, on the bonding curve and later in the graduated pool. That fee is split between the launchpad protocol and the creator side. On Feeverage the whole creator side goes to the position, not to a person.

FeeWho sets itWhere it goes
Base trading feeLaunchpad protocolSplit: protocol share, rest to the position
Extra trade tax (optional)Creator, at launch (capped)100% to the position
Launch feeLaunchpad protocolProtocol, paid once at launch

The extra trade tax is how a creator makes the position grow faster. Traders pay it on top of the base fee, so a high tax also makes the token more expensive to trade.

From fee to position

  1. Accrue. Fees build up inside the token's bonding curve.
  2. Release. They are released to the fee escrow when the curve is swept. That happens at graduation, when the launchpad sweeps, or when the creator presses Push fees on the token.
  3. Claim. The keeper claims released ETH from the escrow every few minutes and credits each token by its own fee events.
  4. Bridge. Once a token has at least ~$12 waiting, the keeper bridges its ETH to USDC on Hyperliquid, straight into that token's account.
  5. Trade. The new margin is levered into the token's market and side.
Bridging usually lands in under a minute, but can take longer when the bridge is busy. Fees still waiting are shown as Fees not yet bridged on each token.

Hyperliquid accounts

Every token gets its own Hyperliquid account, so positions never mix. The address is listed on the token's card and in the trade panel. Open it on Hyperliquid's explorer to see the margin, the entry price, every fill and the funding paid.

Liquidation

With leverage L, a fresh position is liquidated after a move against it of roughly:

distance ≈ 1 ÷ L − maintenance margin
LeverageApprox. move to liquidation
2×≈ 49%
5×≈ 19%
10×≈ 9%
20×≈ 4%

Because size is added over time, the average entry moves and the real liquidation price drifts. The live figure is on each token's card. After a liquidation the account is empty; fees that arrive later open a fresh position with the same settings.

Curve & graduation

A new token trades on a constant-product bonding curve priced in ETH. The progress bar on each card shows how much ETH the curve has taken in against its graduation threshold. When it fills, the curve graduates: its reserves seed a full-range Uniswap v4 pool on Robinhood Chain and the liquidity is locked permanently. Fees keep flowing to the position after graduation.

Before graduation you can buy and sell directly on Feeverage from the token's Trade panel. After graduation, trade it in any Robinhood Chain swap app with the contract address.

Risks

  • Leverage. Positions can be liquidated and the margin lost.
  • Custody. Fees pass through the Feeverage fee recipient and keeper, and the Hyperliquid accounts are controlled by Feeverage's keeper key.
  • Bridging. Fees cross chains through a third-party bridge, which can be delayed.
  • Smart contracts. Bugs in any contract involved can lose funds. Nothing here is audited by Feeverage.
  • Memecoins. Token prices can go to zero regardless of how the position does.
Feeverage is an experiment. Nothing here is financial advice. Only use money you can afford to lose.

FAQ

Does the position's profit go to holders?

Not automatically. The position is public and tied to the token, so it is part of the token's story, but profits stay in the token's Hyperliquid account.

Can the creator change the market or leverage later?

No. The strategy line is written into the token at launch and the keeper follows it.

Can the creator take the fees?

No. The creator side of the fee goes to the Feeverage fee recipient, which only funds positions.

Why does a token show "Waiting for fees"?

Its fees are still on the curve, still being bridged, or below Hyperliquid's $10 minimum order.

Which wallets work?

MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, anything with WalletConnect, or an email / Google / X login with a Privy embedded wallet. You need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain.
