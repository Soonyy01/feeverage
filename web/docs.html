Feeverage docs

Feeverage is a token launchpad on BNB Chain, built on flap.sh. Every token launched here is wired to one leveraged perpetual position on Hyperliquid. Trading the token pays a tax; the tax becomes margin; the margin is levered into the market the creator picked at launch.

In one line: your token's fees, leveraged. $1 of fees at 5× becomes about $4.75 of exposure.

Getting started

  1. Press Connect wallet in the top-right corner, next to the Menu. You can sign in with MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, WalletConnect, or with email, Google or X. If you sign in without a wallet, one is created for you.
  2. Make sure the wallet has some BNB on BNB Chain for gas and your optional first buy.
  3. Choose Launch in the menu to create a token, or scroll the home page to browse and trade existing ones.

The menu also switches the language (English / 中文) and the theme (light / dark). Both choices are remembered on your device.

Launching a token

  1. Describe the token. Name, ticker, a logo picked from your gallery, a description and optional socials (X, Telegram, website). The logo and description are pinned on IPFS through flap.sh, so wallets, explorers and flap.sh show them too. If you skip the logo, one is drawn from the ticker.
  2. Pick the position. A Hyperliquid market (BTC, ETH, BNB, HYPE, SOL, XRP or DOGE), long or short, and a leverage from 1× to 20×. Leverage is also capped by the market's own maximum on Hyperliquid.
  3. Optional first buy. Your first buy happens in the same transaction as the launch, so nobody can buy before you. Leave it empty to launch without buying.
  4. Pick the trade tax. 1%, 3%, 5% or 10% on every buy and sell. All of it funds the position, so a higher tax grows the position faster but makes the token more expensive to trade.
  5. Confirm. The site pins the metadata, finds the token address (flap.sh tax tokens end in 7777) and simulates the transaction, so an error shows up before you pay any gas. One transaction creates the token on flap.sh and your first buy. You pay your first buy and gas.

The strategy line

Feeverage appends one line to your description that records the position, for example:

Fees → 5x LONG BTC on Hyperliquid · feeverage:BTC:L:5

Because it is part of the token's pinned metadata, the strategy is public and can never be changed after launch. The keeper reads this line to know what to trade.

The position

Each token has exactly one position: the market, side and leverage chosen at launch. Every time new fees arrive, the keeper adds them as margin and opens more size in the same direction.

new size = deposit × 0.95 × leverage ÷ price

5% of every deposit stays aside to cover Hyperliquid trading fees and funding, so a fresh deposit does not sit right at the liquidation edge. Positions use cross margin: all the margin in the token's account backs the position.

  • Orders are immediate-or-cancel limit orders at most 1% through the current price.
  • Hyperliquid's minimum order is $10 of notional, so very small amounts wait until they add up.
  • Feeverage never closes a position. It runs until it is liquidated.

Fees

Every buy and sell of a Feeverage token pays the trade tax chosen at launch, on the flap.sh bonding curve and later on PancakeSwap. flap.sh keeps a small protocol share and pays the rest in BNB to the Feeverage fee recipient. On Feeverage the whole tax goes to the position, never to a person.

FeeWho sets itWhere it goes
flap.sh trading feeflap.shflap.sh, 1% on the bonding curve
Trade tax (1–10%)Creator, at launchThe position (after flap.sh's protocol share)
Bridge + Hyperliquid feesBridge and HyperliquidTaken from the token's own fees

From fee to position

  1. Collect. Every trade pays the tax. flap.sh converts it to BNB and sends it to the Feeverage fee recipient.
  2. Book. Every few minutes the keeper reads how much BNB each token has sent and credits it to that token, so every token's money stays separate.
  3. Bridge. Once a token has at least $12 waiting, the keeper bridges that BNB to USDC on Hyperliquid, straight into the token's account. On each token this shows as Next top-up $x / $12.
  4. Trade. The new margin is levered into the token's market and side.
A bridge usually lands within a minute, but can take longer when the bridge is busy.

Live numbers

Prices on Feeverage are live. The ticker under the header streams Hyperliquid prices for every supported market and flashes green or red on each change. If Hyperliquid cannot be reached from your network, prices fall back to CoinGecko and Binance so they keep moving.

Every open position is re-marked against the live price of its own market every second:

live PnL = size × (live price − entry price)

For a short, size is negative, so the same formula gives profit when the price falls. Margin, notional, the health gauge and the totals at the top of the home page all update from the same live price.

Hyperliquid accounts

Every token gets its own Hyperliquid account, so positions never mix. The address is shown on the token page. Open it on Hyperliquid's explorer to check the margin, the entry price, every fill and the funding paid.

Liquidation & health

With leverage L, a fresh position is liquidated after a move against it of roughly:

distance ≈ 1 ÷ L − maintenance margin
LeverageApprox. move to liquidation
2×≈ 49%
5×≈ 19%
10×≈ 9%
20×≈ 4%

The token page shows a live health gauge: Safe above 25% from liquidation, Healthy above 12%, Tight above 5%, and Danger below that. Because size is added over time, the average entry moves and the real liquidation price drifts. After a liquidation the account is empty; fees that arrive later open a fresh position with the same settings.

Curve & graduation

A new token trades on its flap.sh bonding curve, priced in BNB. The token page shows how far the curve has filled. When it reaches 80%, the token lists on PancakeSwap. The trade tax keeps applying there, so fees keep flowing to the position after listing.

Buying & selling

Before graduation you can trade any token directly on its page: choose Buy or Sell, enter an amount and confirm in your wallet. The page shows a quote first and protects you with a 3% slippage limit. Selling asks for a one-time token approval. Trades go through the flap.sh Portal. After the token lists, trade it on PancakeSwap using its contract address.

Token list

The home page lists every Feeverage token. Sort by Newest, Live positions, Top PnL, Top MCAP, Most funded, Next top-up or Graduated, filter by All, Long or Short, and search by ticker, name, market or address. Each card shows the strategy, live PnL, market cap, funding and progress to the next top-up. Tap the address to copy it.

Your positions

Once your wallet is connected, the wallet button shows your address. Open it, or choose Positions in the menu, to see every Feeverage token you hold.

  • BNB balance of your wallet on BNB Chain.
  • Balance of each token, read from the token contract.
  • Price of each token, read live from flap.sh, refreshed every 10 seconds.
  • Average entry, from the buys you made on Feeverage from this browser.
  • Value and PnL in USD, re-marked every second with the live BNB price, plus how far the token has moved since your entry.
  • Your trade history, with a link to every transaction.

Entry price and PnL count trades made on Feeverage from this browser. Tokens you received another way show their value but no entry price.

Risks

  • Leverage. Positions can be liquidated and the margin lost.
  • Custody. Fees pass through the Feeverage fee recipient and keeper, and the Hyperliquid accounts are controlled by the keeper key.
  • Bridging. Fees cross chains through a third-party bridge, which can be delayed.
  • Smart contracts. Bugs in any contract involved can lose funds. Nothing here has been audited by Feeverage.
  • Memecoins. A token's price can go to zero no matter how its position does.
Feeverage is an experiment. Nothing here is financial advice. Only use money you can afford to lose.

FAQ

Does the position's profit go to holders?

Not automatically. The position is public and tied to the token, so it is part of the token's story, but profits stay in the token's Hyperliquid account.

Can the creator change the market or leverage later?

No. The strategy line is written into the token at launch and the keeper follows it.

Can the creator take the fees?

No. The tax goes to the Feeverage fee recipient, which only funds positions.

Why does a token say "Opens at $12"?

Its fees have not reached the $12 top-up threshold yet, or they are still being bridged.

Why do my numbers change every second?

PnL, margin and notional are re-marked against the live price of the token's market on every tick.

Feeverage 文档

Feeverage 是 BNB Chain 上基于 flap.sh 的代币发射平台。在这里发射的每个代币都连接着 Hyperliquid 上的一个杠杆永续仓位。交易代币会支付交易税；交易税变成保证金；保证金按创建者在发射时选定的市场加上杠杆。

一句话：你的代币手续费，加杠杆。$1 手续费在 5× 杠杆下约等于 $4.75 的敞口。
组成部分所在位置作用
代币 + 联合曲线BNB Chain（flap.sh）从第一笔交易开始定价。曲线填满后上线 PancakeSwap。
手续费接收地址BNB Chain以 BNB 接收所有 Feeverage 代币的交易税。
KeeperFeeverage 服务器按代币记账手续费、把 BNB 跨链换成 USDC、下单、发布实时数据。
仓位账户Hyperliquid每个代币一个账户，存放保证金和仓位。

快速开始

  1. 点击右上角 菜单 旁边的 连接钱包。可以使用 MetaMask、Rabby、Coinbase Wallet、OKX Wallet、WalletConnect，也可以用邮箱、Google 或 X 登录。没有钱包的话会自动为你创建一个。
  2. 确保钱包里有 BNB Chain 上的 BNB，用于支付 gas 和可选的首笔买入。
  3. 在菜单中选择 发射 来创建代币，或者在首页浏览和交易已有的代币。

菜单还可以切换语言（English / 中文）和主题（浅色 / 深色），你的选择会保存在当前设备上。

发射代币

  1. 填写代币信息。名称、代码、从相册选择的 logo、简介，以及可选的社交链接（X、Telegram、网站）。logo 和简介会通过 flap.sh 固定在 IPFS 上，钱包、浏览器和 flap.sh 都能显示。如果不选 logo，会根据代码自动生成一个。
  2. 选择仓位。Hyperliquid 市场（BTC、ETH、BNB、HYPE、SOL、XRP 或 DOGE）、做多或做空，以及 1× 到 20× 的杠杆。杠杆同时受该市场在 Hyperliquid 上的最大杠杆限制。
  3. 可选的首笔买入。首笔买入与发射在同一笔交易中完成，所以没人能抢在你之前买入。留空则只发射不买入。
  4. 选择交易税。每次买入和卖出收取 1%、3%、5% 或 10%，全部用于仓位。税率越高仓位增长越快，但代币的交易成本也越高。
  5. 确认。网站会先固定元数据、生成代币地址（flap.sh 税收代币地址以 7777 结尾）并模拟交易，如果会失败，你在支付 gas 之前就能看到错误。一笔交易即可在 flap.sh 上创建代币并完成首笔买入。你只需支付首笔买入金额和 gas。

策略说明

Feeverage 会在简介末尾自动添加一行，记录这个代币的仓位，例如：

Fees → 5x LONG BTC on Hyperliquid · feeverage:BTC:L:5

这一行属于代币固定在 IPFS 上的元数据，所以策略是公开的，发射后永远无法更改。Keeper 读取这一行来决定交易什么。

仓位

每个代币只有一个仓位：发射时选定的市场、方向和杠杆。每当有新的手续费到账，keeper 就把它作为保证金，并在同一方向上加仓。

新增规模 = 存入金额 × 0.95 × 杠杆 ÷ 价格

每笔存入的 5% 预留给 Hyperliquid 交易手续费和资金费，避免新仓位一开就贴近清算线。仓位使用 全仓保证金：代币账户里的全部保证金都用来支撑这个仓位。

  • 订单为立即成交或取消（IOC）限价单，偏离当前价格最多 1%。
  • Hyperliquid 最小订单名义价值为 $10，金额太小时会等累积够了再下单。
  • Feeverage 永远不会主动平仓，仓位会一直持有直到被清算。

手续费

Feeverage 代币的每一次买入和卖出都会支付发射时选定的交易税，先在 flap.sh 联合曲线上，之后在 PancakeSwap 上。flap.sh 保留一小部分协议份额，其余以 BNB 支付给 Feeverage 的手续费接收地址。在 Feeverage 上，交易税全部进入仓位，永远不会进入任何个人的口袋。

费用由谁设定去向
flap.sh 交易手续费flap.shflap.sh，联合曲线上 1%
交易税（1–10%）创建者，发射时设定仓位（扣除 flap.sh 协议份额后）
跨链 + Hyperliquid 费用跨链桥和 Hyperliquid从代币自身的手续费中扣除

从手续费到仓位

  1. 收取。每笔交易都会支付交易税。flap.sh 将其换成 BNB 并发送到 Feeverage 的手续费接收地址。
  2. 记账。Keeper 每隔几分钟读取每个代币已发送的 BNB，并记到对应代币上，确保每个代币的资金互不混合。
  3. 跨链。某个代币待处理的手续费达到 $12 后，keeper 会把这些 BNB 跨链换成 Hyperliquid 上的 USDC，直接存入该代币的账户。每个代币上都会显示为 下次加仓 $x / $12。
  4. 交易。新的保证金按代币设定的市场和方向加上杠杆。
跨链通常一分钟内到账，但在拥堵时可能更久。

实时数据

Feeverage 上的价格都是实时的。页头下方的行情条实时显示所有支持市场的 Hyperliquid 价格，每次变动都会闪烁绿色或红色。如果你的网络无法访问 Hyperliquid，价格会自动改用 CoinGecko 和 Binance 的数据，保持实时更新。

每个持仓每秒都会根据其所在市场的实时价格重新计算：

实时盈亏 = 仓位规模 × (实时价格 − 开仓价)

做空时仓位规模为负数，所以同一公式在价格下跌时得到盈利。保证金、名义价值、健康度指示和首页的汇总数据都基于同一个实时价格更新。

已导入手续费支付给仓位的交易税，以 BNB 和美元计。
已投入保证金所有代币 Hyperliquid 账户的当前价值。
持仓名义价值所有持仓按实时价格计算的总规模。
未实现盈亏所有持仓按实时价格计算的盈亏。
已注资已跨链注入某个代币仓位的手续费（美元）。
市值代币在曲线上的价格 × 总供应量，以美元计。

Hyperliquid 账户

每个代币都有独立的 Hyperliquid 账户，仓位永不混合。账户地址显示在代币页面上。在 Hyperliquid 浏览器中打开即可查看保证金、开仓价、每一笔成交和已支付的资金费。

清算与健康度

使用杠杆 L 时，新仓位在价格反向波动大约以下幅度后会被清算：

距离 ≈ 1 ÷ L − 维持保证金
杠杆距清算的大致波动幅度
2×≈ 49%
5×≈ 19%
10×≈ 9%
20×≈ 4%

代币页面会实时显示 健康度：距清算超过 25% 为 安全，超过 12% 为 健康，超过 5% 为 紧张，低于 5% 为 危险。由于仓位会不断加仓，平均开仓价和实际清算价都会变化。清算后账户清空；之后到账的手续费会以相同设置开新仓。

曲线与毕业

新代币在 flap.sh 的联合曲线上交易，以 BNB 计价。代币页面会显示曲线的填充进度。达到 80% 时，代币上线 PancakeSwap。交易税在那里继续生效，所以上线后手续费仍会流向仓位。

买入与卖出

毕业前，你可以直接在代币页面交易：选择 买入 或 卖出，输入数量，然后在钱包中确认。页面会先显示报价，并以 3% 的滑点上限保护你。卖出前需要一次性授权代币。交易通过 flap.sh Portal 完成。代币上线后，请使用合约地址在 PancakeSwap 上交易。

风险

  • 杠杆。仓位可能被清算，保证金全部损失。
  • 托管。手续费会经过 Feeverage 的手续费接收地址和 keeper，Hyperliquid 账户由 keeper 私钥控制。
  • 跨链。手续费通过第三方跨链桥转移，可能出现延迟。
  • 智能合约。任何相关合约中的漏洞都可能导致资金损失。Feeverage 尚未进行审计。
  • Meme 币。无论仓位表现如何，代币价格都可能归零。
Feeverage 是一个实验性项目，本页内容不构成投资建议。请只投入你能承受损失的资金。

常见问题

仓位的盈利会分给持有者吗？

不会自动分配。仓位是公开的，并与代币绑定，是代币故事的一部分，但盈利留在该代币的 Hyperliquid 账户中。

创建者之后可以更改市场或杠杆吗？

不可以。策略在发射时写入代币，keeper 严格按此执行。

创建者可以拿走手续费吗？

不可以。交易税进入 Feeverage 的手续费接收地址，只用于为仓位注资。

为什么代币显示“满 $12 开仓”？

它的手续费还没达到 $12 的加仓门槛，或者正在跨链。

为什么数据每秒都在变化？

每次价格变动时，盈亏、保证金和名义价值都会按代币所在市场的实时价格重新计算。
