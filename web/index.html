How it works

Read the docs
↑

Launch

Name it, pick a Hyperliquid market, a side and a leverage. The token goes live on a bonding curve on Robinhood Chain in one transaction.

⇄

Trade

Every buy and sell pays a fee. The creator share of that fee is routed to Feeverage instead of a wallet.

⤳

Bridge

The keeper claims the ETH, bridges it to USDC and deposits it into the token's own Hyperliquid account.

×

Lever up

Each deposit is added to the position at the chosen leverage. Bigger volume, bigger position.
